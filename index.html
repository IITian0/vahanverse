The problem

India has the demand data. It cannot see the supply.

Two asymmetric systems that no one has put on the same map.

What exists

VAHAN records every EV registration.

By RTO office, vehicle category and fuel type — public, granular and updated regularly. The strongest India-specific demand signal there is.

What is missing

One national view of installed chargers.

Chargers sit across operator apps and state portals, so no one can say which districts are underserved — or what each region will need next.

The platform

Four layers, one national view.

API-first, so every layer can be extended as better data arrives.

01

Harmonisation

Ingest, clean and de-duplicate every source, then project all of it onto one H3 hexagon grid.

02

Analytics

The Charging Adequacy Index, gap classification and demand models across every cell.

03

Decision

Rank regions by investment priority and simulate where the next chargers should go.

04

Interface

A national GIS map and a planner dashboard anyone can read and act on.

How it works

From scattered sources to one grid.

01

Ingest & standardise

One schema for every source.

02

Geocode & validate

Checked against boundaries.

03

De-duplicate

One charger listed twice becomes one.

04

Aggregate to grid

Rolled up to H3 cells.

05

Map RTO to geography

Registrations land on the same cells.

The app

A working national console.

Hover any state for its ratio, click for detail, and drag the deployment slider to simulate new chargers — the map, the KPIs and the priority list update live.
